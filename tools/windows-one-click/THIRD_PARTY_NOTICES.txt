LeadBridge KSO Windows one-click installer — third-party components

The installer includes unmodified installers, portable runtimes or binary data from:

1. Python 3.12.10 (Python Software Foundation License)
   https://www.python.org/downloads/release/python-31210/

2. Pillow 12.3.0 (HPND License)
   https://pypi.org/project/pillow/12.3.0/

3. Tesseract OCR 5.5.3 (Apache License 2.0)
   https://github.com/tesseract-ocr/tesseract/releases/tag/5.5.3

4. tessdata_fast rus/eng models (Apache License 2.0)
   https://github.com/tesseract-ocr/tessdata_fast/tree/87416418657359cb625c412a48b6e1d6d41c29bd

The dependency URLs and SHA-256 hashes are pinned in build_setup.py.
Python, Pillow and Tesseract are private LeadBridge runtime components under C:\LeadBridgeKSO\runtime and are removed by the LeadBridge uninstaller.
